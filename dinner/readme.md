# Steak Frites

## Description

Steak frites is a classic French bistro dish: a pan-seared steak served with crispy double-fried fries. It's easy to make in bigger batches and serve family-style, which makes it a great dinner for groups.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 4 steaks (sirloin, ribeye, or hanger), about 8 oz each and 1 inch thick
- [ ] 2 lbs russet potatoes
- [ ] About 6 cups neutral oil for frying (canola or peanut), plus 1 tbsp for the pan
- [ ] 6 tbsp unsalted butter, divided (4 tbsp softened)
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves (1 minced, 2 smashed)
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] Black pepper
- [ ] Optional: Dijon mustard or mayonnaise for dipping

### Steps for preparation:

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into sticks about 1/4 inch thick. Soak them in a bowl of cold water for 30 minutes, then drain and pat them completely dry with a towel.
2. While the potatoes soak, take the steaks out of the fridge. Pat them dry with paper towels and season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
3. Make the herb butter: in a small bowl, mix the 4 tbsp softened butter with the minced garlic, parsley, and a pinch of salt. Set aside.
4. Heat the frying oil in a large heavy pot to 325°F (use a thermometer). Fry the potatoes in small batches for 4 to 5 minutes, until soft but still pale. Move them to a tray lined with paper towels.
5. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat until it just starts to smoke. Add the steaks and cook 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare (130°F inside).
6. Turn the heat to medium. Add the remaining 2 tbsp butter, the smashed garlic, and the thyme to the pan. Tilt the pan and spoon the melted butter over the steaks for about 1 minute.
7. Move the steaks to a cutting board and let them rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
8. While the steaks rest, raise the frying oil to 375°F. Fry the potatoes a second time, in batches, for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crispy. Drain and salt them right away.
9. Slice the steaks against the grain, top each with a spoonful of herb butter, and serve with a pile of fries.

### Beverage Pairing:
**Pomegranate Rosemary Spritz** (non-alcoholic)
- 3 oz 100% pomegranate juice, chilled
- ½ tbsp fresh lime juice
- 4 oz soda water, chilled
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- Ice

Lightly bruise one rosemary sprig and drop it into a glass with the pomegranate and lime juice. Let it sit for 2 minutes, then fill with ice, top with soda water, and garnish with the second sprig. The tart pomegranate cuts the richness of the steak, and the rosemary picks up the herb butter.
### Notes:

- Oven fries option: toss the dried potato sticks with 2 tbsp oil and salt, then bake at 425°F for 30 to 35 minutes, flipping halfway.
- Doneness guide: 130°F medium-rare, 140°F medium, 150°F medium-well.
- Never leave hot oil unattended, and keep a lid nearby in case of a flare-up.
