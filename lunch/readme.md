Chicken Pad Thai (serves 4)

INGREDIENTS

8 oz flat rice noodles (about 1/4 inch wide)
12 oz boneless chicken thighs or breast, thinly sliced
3 tbsp tamarind paste (concentrate)
3 tbsp fish sauce
3 tbsp brown or palm sugar
1 tsp sriracha or chili flakes (optional)
3 tbsp neutral oil
3 garlic cloves, minced
1 shallot, thinly sliced
2 large eggs
2 cups bean sprouts
4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
1/3 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
2 limes, cut into wedges
1/4 cup fresh cilantro, for garnish

STEPS

Soak the noodles in warm (not boiling) water until pliable but still firm, about 25 to 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
Whisk together the tamarind, fish sauce, sugar, and sriracha until the sugar dissolves. It should taste punchy and balanced between sour, salty, and sweet.
Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Stir-fry the chicken until cooked through and lightly browned, about 5 minutes. Move to a plate.
Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil, then the garlic and shallot. Stir-fry until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
Push the aromatics to one side, crack in the eggs, and scramble until just set.
Add the drained noodles and the sauce. Toss constantly until the noodles absorb the sauce and turn tender, about 3 minutes. If they seem dry or stiff, add 1 to 2 tbsp water.
Return the chicken to the pan with the bean sprouts and green onions. Toss for about 1 minute, just until the onions wilt and the sprouts stay crunchy.
Top with peanuts and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.

NOTES

Keep the noodles slightly firm after soaking; they finish softening in the pan, and over-soaked noodles turn mushy.
If your pan is small, cook in two batches so everything sears instead of steaming.
Tamarind concentrate varies in strength, so start with less and adjust to taste.
No tamarind? Use lime juice plus a little rice vinegar.
Shrimp or tofu swap in easily for the chicken.
