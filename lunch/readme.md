# Chicken Pad Thai

## Description

Chewy stir-fried rice noodles with chicken, egg, bean sprouts, and peanuts in a sour, salty, and sweet tamarind sauce.

**Historical context:** Pad thai means "Thai-style stir-fried noodles," and the noodles themselves trace back to southern Chinese immigrants; its original name, kuai tiao phat Thai, borrows the Teochew word for flat rice noodles. The dish was popularized in the late 1930s and 1940s under Prime Minister Plaek Phibunsongkhram, whose government promoted rice noodles to reduce domestic rice consumption, built a sense of national identity around the dish, and distributed a standardized recipe to street vendors. Its exact origins are still debated, but within a few years it spread across the country and later became Thailand's best-known dish abroad.

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 8 oz flat rice noodles (about 1/4 inch wide)
- [ ] 12 oz boneless chicken thighs or breast, thinly sliced
- [ ] 3 tbsp tamarind paste (concentrate)
- [ ] 3 tbsp fish sauce
- [ ] 3 tbsp brown or palm sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp sriracha or chili flakes (optional)
- [ ] 3 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 shallot, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 2 cups bean sprouts
- [ ] 4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
- [ ] 1/3 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
- [ ] 2 limes, cut into wedges
- [ ] 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, for garnish

### Steps for preparation:

1. **Soak the noodles:** Soak the noodles in warm (not boiling) water until pliable but still firm, about 25 to 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
2. **Make the sauce:** Whisk together the tamarind, fish sauce, sugar, and sriracha until the sugar dissolves. It should taste punchy and balanced between sour, salty, and sweet.
3. **Cook the chicken:** Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Stir-fry the chicken until cooked through and lightly browned, about 5 minutes. Move to a plate.
4. **Cook the aromatics:** Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil, then the garlic and shallot. Stir-fry until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Scramble the eggs:** Push the aromatics to one side, crack in the eggs, and scramble until just set.
6. **Add noodles and sauce:** Add the drained noodles and the sauce. Toss constantly until the noodles absorb the sauce and turn tender, about 3 minutes. If they seem dry or stiff, add 1 to 2 tbsp water.
7. **Combine:** Return the chicken to the pan with the bean sprouts and green onions. Toss for about 1 minute, just until the onions wilt and the sprouts stay crunchy.
8. **Garnish and serve:** Top with peanuts and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.

### Notes:

- Keep the noodles slightly firm after soaking; they finish softening in the pan, and over-soaked noodles turn mushy.
- If your pan is small, cook in two batches so everything sears instead of steaming.
- Tamarind concentrate varies in strength, so start with less and adjust to taste.
- No tamarind? Use lime juice plus a little rice vinegar.
- Shrimp or tofu swap in easily for the chicken.
