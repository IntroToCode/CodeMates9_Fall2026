# Cheese Board-Style Pizza

## Description

A thin, crispy sourdough pizza topped with mozzarella, feta, and fresh vegetables, finished with a bright cilantro-lime green sauce. It's inspired by Cheese Board Pizza in North Berkeley, our team's favorite lunch near campus.

**Historical context:** The Cheese Board opened in 1967 as a small cheese shop in North Berkeley and later became a worker-owned cooperative. It's now famous for baking just one vegetarian pizza each day, with toppings that change daily, and for the long lines down Shattuck Avenue that come with it.

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 1 lb pizza dough (sourdough if you can find it), at room temperature
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1½ cups shredded mozzarella
- [ ] ½ cup crumbled feta
- [ ] 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- [ ] 1 small zucchini, thinly sliced
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1 cup fresh cilantro, loosely packed (green sauce)
- [ ] 1 jalapeño, seeded (green sauce)
- [ ] 1 clove garlic (green sauce)
- [ ] Juice of 1 lime (green sauce)
- [ ] ¼ cup olive oil (green sauce)
- [ ] Pinch of salt (green sauce)

### Steps for preparation:

1. **Preheat the oven:** Put a baking sheet or pizza stone in the oven and preheat to 500°F for at least 30 minutes.
2. **Make the green sauce:** Blend the cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, lime juice, olive oil, and salt until smooth. Set aside.
3. **Shape the dough:** On a sheet of parchment paper, stretch the dough into a 12-inch circle.
4. **Add the garlic oil:** Mix the olive oil and minced garlic, then brush it over the dough.
5. **Top the pizza:** Sprinkle on the mozzarella, then add the onion, tomatoes, and zucchini. Finish with feta, salt, and pepper.
6. **Bake:** Slide the pizza (on its parchment) onto the hot sheet or stone. Bake 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
7. **Serve:** Let it cool for 2 minutes, slice, and serve with green sauce for drizzling.

### Notes:

- Cheese Board changes its toppings every day, so feel free to swap in other vegetables like mushrooms, corn, or bell peppers.
- Dough that has sat at room temperature for 30 minutes is much easier to stretch.
- Leftover green sauce keeps in the fridge for about 3 days.
