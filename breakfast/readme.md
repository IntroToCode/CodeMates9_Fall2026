# Medialunas de Manteca (Argentine Butter Croissants)

## Description

Small, sweet, syrup-glazed Argentine croissants, the classic partner to café con leche or mate.

## Stats

| Field | Info |
|:---:|:---:|
| Prep Time | 1 hour active (plus about 3 hours chilling and proofing) |
| Cook Time | 15 minutes |
| Total Time | about 4 hours 15 minutes |
| Servings | about 24 medialunas |
| Difficulty | Hard |
| Tags | breakfast, Argentine, pastry, laminated dough, make-ahead, freezer-friendly |

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 500 grams all-purpose flour
- [ ] 80 grams sugar
- [ ] 1 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 10 grams instant yeast
- [ ] 200 milliliters whole milk, lukewarm
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 1 tablespoon honey
- [ ] 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- [ ] 50 grams unsalted butter, softened (for dough)
- [ ] 200 grams unsalted butter, cold (for laminating)
- [ ] 1 large egg, beaten (for egg wash)
- [ ] 100 grams sugar (for syrup)
- [ ] 100 milliliters water (for syrup)

### Steps for preparation:

1. **Make the dough:** Mix 500 grams all-purpose flour, 80 grams sugar, 1 teaspoon salt, and 10 grams instant yeast. Add 200 milliliters whole milk, lukewarm, 2 large eggs, 1 tablespoon honey, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, then knead in 50 grams unsalted butter, softened (for dough) until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes by hand or 5 in a mixer.
2. **Chill the dough:** Flatten the dough into a rectangle, wrap, and refrigerate.
3. **Shape the butter block:** Pound 200 grams unsalted butter, cold (for laminating) between parchment into a flat square about 15 cm (6 in) wide. Keep it cold but pliable.
4. **Laminate (3 folds):** Roll the dough into a rectangle twice the size of the butter. Enclose the butter, roll out to a long rectangle, and fold in thirds like a letter. Wrap and chill 30 minutes. Repeat for three folds total.
5. **Cut and shape:** Roll the dough to about 4 mm thick. Cut long, narrow triangles (base about 8 cm). Stretch each gently, roll from base to tip, and curve the ends into a crescent. Place on parchment-lined trays.
6. **Proof:** Cover loosely and proof in a warm spot until puffy and nearly doubled.
7. **Bake:** Heat the oven to 200°C (390°F). Brush with 1 large egg, beaten (for egg wash) and bake until deep golden.
8. **Make the syrup:** While they bake, boil 100 grams sugar (for syrup) and 100 milliliters water (for syrup) until slightly thickened.
9. **Glaze:** Brush the hot syrup over the medialunas as soon as they leave the oven. Serve warm.

### Notes:

- Keep the butter and dough at similar firmness during lamination; if butter starts leaking, chill longer.
- Shaped, unproofed medialunas freeze well: thaw overnight in the fridge, then proof and bake.
- For medialunas de grasa, swap the laminating butter for beef tallow, cut the sugar in the dough to about 20 g, and skip the syrup.
