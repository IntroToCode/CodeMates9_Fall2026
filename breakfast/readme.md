Breakfast: Medialunas de Manteca (Argentine Butter Croissants) Small, sweet, syrup-glazed Argentine croissants, the classic partner to café con leche or mate.
INGREDIENTS • 500 grams all-purpose flour • 80 grams sugar • 1 teaspoons salt • 10 grams instant yeast • 200 milliliters whole milk, lukewarm • 2 large eggs • 1 tablespoons honey • 1 teaspoons vanilla extract • 50 grams unsalted butter, softened (for dough) • 200 grams unsalted butter, cold (for laminating) • 1 large egg, beaten (for egg wash) • 100 grams sugar (for syrup) • 100 milliliters water (for syrup)

STEPS

Make the dough: Mix 500 grams all-purpose flour, 80 grams sugar, 1 teaspoons salt, and 10 grams instant yeast. Add 200 milliliters whole milk, lukewarm, 2 large eggs, 1 tablespoons honey, and 1 teaspoons vanilla extract, then knead in 50 grams unsalted butter, softened (for dough) until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes by hand or 5 in a mixer.
Chill the dough: Flatten the dough into a rectangle, wrap, and refrigerate.
Shape the butter block: Pound 200 grams unsalted butter, cold (for laminating) between parchment into a flat square about 15 cm (6 in) wide. Keep it cold but pliable.
Laminate (3 folds): Roll the dough into a rectangle twice the size of the butter. Enclose the butter, roll out to a long rectangle, and fold in thirds like a letter. Wrap and chill 30 minutes. Repeat for three folds total.
Cut and shape: Roll the dough to about 4 mm thick. Cut long, narrow triangles (base about 8 cm). Stretch each gently, roll from base to tip, and curve the ends into a crescent. Place on parchment-lined trays.
Proof: Cover loosely and proof in a warm spot until puffy and nearly doubled.
Bake: Heat the oven to 200°C (390°F). Brush with 1 large egg, beaten (for egg wash) and bake until deep golden.
Make the syrup: While they bake, boil 100 grams sugar (for syrup) and 100 milliliters water (for syrup) until slightly thickened.
Glaze: Brush the hot syrup over the medialunas as soon as they leave the oven. Serve warm.
NOTES Keep the butter and dough at similar firmness during lamination; if butter starts leaking, chill longer. Shaped, unproofed medialunas freeze well: thaw overnight in the fridge, then proof and bake. For medialunas de grasa, swap the laminating butter for beef tallow, cut the sugar in the dough to about 20 g, and skip the syrup.
